Privacy policy

Woof keeps your dog's records only on your phone. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Your dog's name, photo, breed, vaccine, medication, weight and walk records stay on your device and are never sent to us or to third parties.

Microphone

The translator analyses your dog's voice on your device only, with Apple's sound classifier. Recordings are never uploaded.

Photos and camera

A photo you pick or take is used only on your device, in the profile and memories. Breed suggestions run on the device too.

Ask (care guide)

Answers come from the guide built into the app or, on supported devices, Apple's on-device model. Your questions are not sent to us.

Notifications

Local notifications only, on vaccine, parasite and medication days.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes every record. You can export or delete your records in Settings.
